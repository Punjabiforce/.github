# Punjabiforce

**One Punjab. One Force.**

Punjabiforce Community: a volunteer-led guild for Salesforce and tech professionals, founded by Punjabi professionals and open to all.

## What we do

We run in-person networking and panel events across the UK, free webinars and masterclasses, and mentoring and career coaching. We support career changers breaking into Salesforce and tech, and connect members with mentors, roles and each other.

## Who it's for

Everyone. Whether you are just starting out, switching careers or already established and want to give back, you are welcome here.
